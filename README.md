# Block Party backend

pnpm monorepo. Built brick by brick; each brick ships with tests against a real Postgres.

| Package | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `packages/core` | Pure domain rules (no I/O), safe to run in Workers |
| `packages/db` | Drizzle schema, migrations, and the services that touch Postgres |
| `apps/api` | The HTTP API (Hono): runs on Cloudflare Workers (`src/worker.ts`) and Node for local dev (`src/node.ts`) |
| `apps/rooms` | The live city: a Cloudflare Worker with a Durable Object per room copy and a directory per place (hibernating WebSockets), tested in real workerd via Miniflare |

## Setup

Needs Node 20+, pnpm 10, and Postgres 15 on localhost.

```bash
pnpm install
cp .env.example .env
createdb blockparty
pnpm db:migrate
pnpm test
pnpm --filter @bp/api dev   # http://localhost:8787, refuses to start on an un-migrated database
```

Tests drop and recreate `blockparty_test` on every run (the name must end in `_test`).

## Bricks

1. **GM ledger** (done). Double-entry, append-only, idempotent. GM is only created from `sys:mint` and only destroyed into `sys:burn`, so `supplyStats()` gives the created/destroyed ratio the plan tracks. The database itself refuses unbalanced transactions, balance edits outside the ledger, forged balance chains, negative resident balances, and any edit or delete of history.
2. **Accounts** (done). Guest on first tap behind the 18+ notice; Telegram Mini App sign-in (HMAC `initData`, checked against an independent implementation); Privy claim (ES256 JWT, algorithm pinned). Claiming keeps the guest's GM; signing into an existing resident leaves the guest behind and never merges GM. Opaque session tokens, stored only as SHA-256 hashes, rotated on every sign-in.
3. **Daily habit** (done). One UTC city clock (the bell at 00:00). `sayGm` pays the drip once per city day: 50 GM, or 100 under 100 GM net worth (furniture, decor and wardrobe bought in the last 7 days still count). `recordStreakDay` pays the streak bonus (+5 a day, capped at +25) and is fired by the calls brick on a resident's first call of the day. Hodl Days: 1 at move-in, hold up to 2, a free one every 7-day streak, extras for 150 GM burned; spent only when they save the streak. Streak cards at 7, 30, 100 and 365. Claiming an account now pays the 1,000 GM move-in gift. Tests replay the plan's weekly math (casual +50 bonus a week, active +175).
   - Also fixed a brick 1 guard bug: posting to one account twice in one transaction was wrongly refused (migration 0004).
4. **Create-a-Resident** (done). Claimed accounts move in once: @handle (unique ignoring case, fixed at MVP), name, mix-and-match look (46 parts plus palettes), 3 of 12 traits (free swap once a season), a goal and a kit style. The city deals the backstory and its starter item server-side; the client can't pick it, and the database refuses any later change. Names and handles block impersonation of the cast and staff (leetspeak, homoglyph and spacing tricks included) and crypto scam bait, without blocking real first names. Name cleanup strips invisible, direction-flipping and tag characters (ASCII smuggling) while keeping emoji, skin tones and flags.
5. **Crews** (done). Create a crew (3 to 50 members, one crew per resident), a lead plus up to 2 deputies, invites (a lead or deputy link joins straight away; a member's link becomes a join request; open crews join in one tap), Open tables, removal strictly downward, lead hand-over. The crew pot takes deposits and only ever loses GM by burning; dissolving burns it. The gm chain settles at each bell from roster history (members at that bell, active = gm in the last 7 days, holds at 60% with at least 3), any 3 members from that day can relight a miss until the next bell, and it stores counts only, never who missed. The database enforces the roster limits, one lead, one crew per resident and append-only history.
6. **Homes and Build mode** (done). Moving in sets up a tenement with the free kit already placed and the backstory starter item in storage. 40 GM pieces in the plan's price bands; buying burns the GM under the kinds the broke floor counts, and released prices are pinned by a test. One of each working piece (trophy wall, TV, Slate board, guestbook) per home. Grid placement with rotation, recolour, rug/floor/wall layers and free storage. Earned-only pieces (30-day gm lamp, 100-day neon) come from the streak. The Crew House is bought from the pot once the lead and 2 members approve, and grows rooms at 11 and 26 members. The database refuses item transfers, deletes, and any purchase not backed by a burn of its exact price.
7. **Rent and jobs** (done). Saturday rent of 250 GM at the bell: 225 burns, 25 to the City Purse, which never pays GM out. Sublet after 7 full days away; can't pay means a week on the crew's couch or the bench, never debt; catch-up looks back at most 8 weeks. Mod Shift: 2 a day across every job, one at a time, a server-scored moderation mini-game (the client never sends a score), 10 to 12 GM after 15 real minutes. The away shift: Proof of Neighbour only, a gm in the last 24 hours, 5 GM, takes one of the 2 slots. The Drip Committee report shows GM created vs destroyed on net flows and the median active balance in weeks of income. Tests replay the plan's active week exactly: drip 350, streak 175, shifts 154.
8. **The bell job and the Halving** (done). `runBell` runs each night's due steps once each (settle every crew's gm chain; rent on Saturdays; the Halving at a season's last bell), records them, resumes cleanly after a crash, and serialises runners on an advisory lock. The Halving burns half the excess above 2,000 on every resident balance and crew pot (rounding for the player), once per season, up to 7 days late. A ledger audit checks every Halving burn against the balance it was taken from.
   - Also fixed two deadlocks found here (Halving vs purchase, rent vs purchase) and their root cause: every row lock is now `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, and balances read before posting go through `lockAccounts` in the ledger's order.
9. **The HTTP API** (done). Every player action from bricks 1 to 8 behind session auth, per-IP and per-player rate limits (guest sign-ups capped per IP), JSON-only bodies with a size cap, CORS allowlist, security headers and request ids. GM-moving routes need an `Idempotency-Key`, namespaced per player. One error shape; every service error code maps to a status (enforced by the type checker); 500s never leak internals. Server-only actions (streak days, minting, earned pieces, pot burns) have no route. Internal routes (`/internal/bell`, `/internal/economy`) hide behind a 32+ character secret. `/health` is 503 until every migration is applied.
   - Tests: end-to-end flows, a route-table check (no route without auth unless listed public), and a fuzzer that sends every hostile value through every path parameter and body field of every route, with a fresh crew per route and a guard that fails if it ever loses its session.
10. **Moods and the candle** (done). A play clock (sessions split by an hour away, written at most once a minute) drives Rested (back after 6+ hours, for ~90 minutes) and Chart-brain (90 minutes without an hour's break; hides the crowd bars and shows the reality check; only time away clears it). Event moods (Called It, REKT, Tilted, Cozy, a nap's Rested) are granted server-side by later bricks through `grantMood`, `maybeTilt` and `endCozy`. Every REKT brings a free Copium; otherwise Copium is 30 GM burned, and is never spent with nothing to clear. An hour of Cozy clears REKT and Tilted (Copium Regular: REKT in 30 minutes; no trait ever touches Tilted or Chart-brain). Tilted caps GM stakes at 10, blocks Snaps and share prompts, and pauses real trades until the next bell even after it's cleared. The candle shows the newest mood; its colour can be hidden.
11. **City Hall: the weekly Mayor and the Law of the Week** (done). Candidates declare Monday to Thursday (Proof of Neighbour, pitch through the scam filter), the booth is open Friday 00:00 to Sunday 23:00 UTC, the Monday bell counts and swears in. One neighbour, one vote, in person (a presence hook; refused until live rooms exist), secret: a vote is a counter bump plus a "you voted" row, never a ballot row, and counts stay hidden until the polls close. Ties go to whoever declared first; no candidates means no Mayor. The Mayor picks one law a week from a menu that can never touch rent, stamps, Tower Lights, the drip, furniture prices, caps or real trades (enforced by a test); Copium Subsidy really discounts Copium.
12. **Moderation and safety** (done). Every message gets checked before sending: seed phrases (BIP39 with checksum, distinctness and typo rules; measured 99.96% of real phrases caught, 0.04% false positives on adversarial chat) and private keys are blocked with the "nobody will ever ask" line; explorer transaction links pass. Links: none from accounts under 7 days, then an allowlist only, read through disguises (dot com, hxxp, full-width dots, zero-width splits). First DMs carry no links or wallet addresses. Crypto scam phrases are blocked. After sending, the AI check runs in batches (OpenAI's free moderation endpoint, behind a provider interface; a failed check never counts as clean). Block works both ways; mute can expire; reports have the plan's reasons, server-side evidence only, a 24-hour deadline and under-18 priority. Staff restrictions need a written reason the player can read (EU statement of reasons); one appeal each, decided by different staff. New accounts get 5 new DM threads a day. Suspended accounts can read, log out and appeal, nothing else.
13. **Live rooms, frens and chat** (done, in four parts). (a) A pure room engine: 60 per room, away residents counted not seated, destination-only moves, rate-limited chat and emotes, fading bubbles, capped evidence, blocks both ways and one-way mutes applied as messages go out, Cozy in the Crew House, home or a Diner booth; a copy directory that keeps crews together and reserves seats. (b) `apps/rooms`: the engine on Durable Objects with hibernating WebSockets, signed 60-second room tickets, moderation before anyone sees a line, a second tab replacing the first, reconnects back to your copy, and rooms that rebuild after hibernation. (c) Frens (two taps while sharing a room, or crewmates), DMs for frens only with the safety line, Boards with follows and slow mode, crew chat; every post through the pre-send check and the AI queue. (d) The API signs tickets (homes need an open door and no block; Crew Houses are for members), turns room effects into Cozy and moderation items, takes flagged content down where it lives, uses live presence for voting in person, and attaches report evidence from the actual conversation or room copy.
14. **Getting around** (done). Walk (free, ~40 s), the Rollup (5 GM: a bus of 8 that leaves when full or 10 s after its first rider, then 10 s on the road; riders who boarded early arrive early when it fills), or priority (15 GM, ~5 s). Fares burn; the Gas Holiday makes rides free. Everyone starts on the street (Crew Row, homes, Crew Houses); room tickets need you to have arrived in that area, so no teleporting and no voting at City Hall without getting there.
15. **Away life and the recap** (done). Offline, a resident keeps wandering by its traits on a 15-minute clock for up to 7 days, then stays home. Nothing runs in the background: each slot is a pure function of (resident, slot), worked out when someone looks. Residents meet the NPC who lives at a place, and crewmates and frens whose own away windows put them in the same place and slot; meetings are mutual by construction. The recap is template keys plus values (i18n-ready), meetings first. Rooms draw away residents dimmed from `/internal/rooms/away`, refreshed at most every 15 minutes.
16. **Proof of Neighbour** (done). An account 3+ days old, verified by phone (mobile only, one account per number, numbers stored only as keyed hashes; 10-minute codes, 5 tries, rate limits) or a crypto-native proof (wallet history, Coinbase Verification, World ID; each subject backs one account), that has shared a room copy in person for a minute or more with 5+ residents. Visits come from the rooms Worker (away residents never count; a second tab isn't a leave). The mark is set once every box is ticked and kept.
17. **Notifications** (done). A queue in Postgres, sent by a cron every minute as Telegram bot messages (plain text, 5-second timeout). The plan's hard limits, re-checked at send time under a per-resident lock so two dispatchers can't squeeze past them: 3 pushes a day by local day, the same wording never twice in 5 days, nothing in quiet hours (23:00 to 08:00 local by default, re-checked if prefs change), and after 7 ignored pushes one a week, the first with the farewell line, once. Event alerts (faded, Snap, shift done...) and the bell-in-2h ping are opt-in; push off stops everything. Wording only from templates whose blanks are all filled, and nothing with money, wallet or FOMO talk, even smuggled in through a handle (`buy_now`). Failed sends retry with backoff, then stop at 3; blocked bots aren't retried. The daily ping fires at a resident's usual hour (median start hour of the last 14 days) only with a reason (an unprotected streak, something riding), once a day. The crew group bot posts at most 3 times a day, one of each kind, and the lead or deputies can mute kinds.
18. **Naps, free will and the city clock** (done). Naps are the first long action: on any Rested piece placed in your own home (a kit bed works exactly like a Lambo bed), 20 real minutes whether you stay or log off, one long action at a time. Each finishes exactly once, by the minute cron or when you look, as of when it ended (a nap left for hours doesn't hand out fresh Rested). Then Rested and an opt-in ping. Waking early gives nothing, and a nap never clears Chart-brain. Free will is a pure function shared with the client: objects advertise a mood and the traits they suit; REKT or Tilted heads for Cozy; ties spread a crowd across chairs; nothing that spends GM, calls, votes or chats can be advertised. The timetable keeps bell-tied big nights at 00:00 UTC and rotates the rest weekly between Asia's, Europe's and the Americas' evenings, never two in one region the same week.
19. **Best frens, rivals and crew bonds** (done). Best frens: up to 3 picks from your frens (crewmates count). A pick is private, and the badge shows only when it's mutual. A pick goes quiet if you stop being frens and comes back if you do. Rivals: proposed to a fren, accepted by them (or by proposing back), ended by either; 3 each at most, pending included, checked on both sides under locks taken in id order (no deadlocks when two propose to each other). Crew bonds: distinct days you actually shared a room with a current crewmate for a minute or more, in 4 levels (1, 5, 15, 40 days). None of it touches GM. A block now ends everything between two residents: frenship, fren taps, picks and rivalry (before this, a block left a frenship in place).
20. **The ledger in shards** (done). `sys:mint` and `sys:burn` are now 16 shards each, chosen by a stable hash of the other account (one resident always uses the same shard; shard 0 keeps the old id, so history stays put). Supply stats, the Drip Committee report, rent, the Halving and the purchase guard all read every shard. Measured with `pnpm --filter @bp/db bench` (200 residents burning at once, local Postgres): 646 burns/s on one row, 1,818/s sharded.
   - Also fixed a production bug found here: nothing outside the tests created the system accounts, so a fresh production database would have failed its first mint. Migration 0026 now creates them, and a test migrates an empty database to prove it.
21. **Deploy and CI** (done). `wrangler.jsonc` for both Workers (Hyperdrive, the minute cron, Durable Objects on SQLite storage, no secrets in config), each checked by a test against the code it deploys. The API is bundled by `build.mjs` and tested as a bundle on workerd through Miniflare against real Postgres: sign-in to purchase, internal routes and the cron. `pnpm --filter @bp/api smoke` walks a new resident through the real Node server on a migrated database (it caught the missing system accounts). CI runs Postgres, migrations, typecheck and every suite, one package at a time since the db and API suites share the test database.
22. **The daily ping scheduler and Faucet Fren** (done). Every 15 minutes the cron pages through residents who played in the last 14 days and queues the daily ping at each one's usual hour when their streak is alive and unplayed today (15 minutes, so half-hour time zones get their hour; never twice a day). Faucet Fren answers 17 FAQs from a script, with every number built from the game's own constants, a greeting ("gm fren") never mistaken for the question, and every line passing the NPC rules (no wallets, deposits, real trades or dollar amounts; never says what to call).
23. **The Tower, part 1: markets, odds and free calls** (done, on a mock feed until Polymarket lands).
   - **Feed:** an `OddsProvider` interface (Polymarket plugs in; `ODDS_SOURCE=mock` gives a deterministic sample feed). One poller at a time (advisory lock) stores a tick per open market per poll, never two for the same moment. Junk from the feed is skipped, and a dead feed reads "odds paused" and stops new calls. Politics markets are Odds Wall only. A resolved or voided market is final, enforced in the database.
   - **Calls:** you pick a side and a "Chance of YES" on your side of the price. The call waits in the mempool and locks at the first tick after you confirm, within 2 points of what you saw, or it's dropped with "price moved" and the ticket comes back. One per market and no edits: the database refuses any change to a locked call except its Aura, written once, and only at a real tick of the same market.
   - **Limits:** off-slate calls take a ticket (3 a day, stacking to 9, checked under a lock); calls in the last 30 minutes earn half Aura (the Slate is exempt).
   - **Tail and Fade:** a Tail (frens only, 2 a day) copies a side; a Fade takes the other and pings them. Neither works across a block or on the Slate.
   - **The Floor:** World, City (from 10 neighbours) and Crew (from 3 crewmates) medians and the Vibe Gap. On the Slate it's blurred until you lock (Lock to see), and Chart-brain hides it.
   - **Aura:** exact integer math in hundredths, property-tested to reward saying what you really believe.
24. **The Tower, part 2: settling, receipts and the Right List** (done).
   - **Settling:** when a market resolves, every locked call is scored once from the tick it locked on (half in the last 30 minutes; a Tail scores 0; a voided market scores nobody). Racing settlers can't double-score, and the database refuses it anyway.
   - **Streaks:** a locked call records the day's streak, so streaks and their GM bonus finally grow.
   - **Receipts:** a public link per call, signed so it can't be faked or guessed, sealed ("IN THE MEMPOOL") until the bell after settlement, then stamped CALLED IT or REKT with its Aura. Never an amount, a wallet, a user id or whether it was real.
   - **The Right List:** season Aura ÷ (settled calls + 15), revealed results only, 10 settled calls to appear, Tails excluded, rusty after 14 days without a call, and a new season starting from 25% of last season's Aura per call.
25. **The Daily Slate** (done).
   - **Publishing:** one shared puzzle a day, published once (racing publishers agree; the database refuses any later change). It's 1 Lock, 2 Coin Flips, 2 Spicy and 1 Long Shot. Every card is callable for 15+ hours and settles before the next bell, with at least 2 non-crypto, at most 2 from one price ladder, and never politics. The nearest tier fills an empty slot, and the pick is deterministic so every server agrees. Six cards as soon as six fit; a short Slate only after 02:00 UTC.
   - **Calls:** Slate calls take no ticket and earn full Aura to the freeze.
   - **Bell Modifier:** one a day (Contrarian Day, NOTHING Day, Blind Slate); Blind Slate hides your crew's bar even after you lock.
   - **The grid:** sealed until the bell, then coloured by Aura (🟩 ⬜ 🟥 ⬛, 🟨 for a Long Shot YES that hit, grey for a voided card, ⏳ for one not settled by the bell), plus the share line ("Block Party #5 🟩⬜🟥⬛🟩🟨") and your city percentile (a tie isn't a win). Calls made before the Slate went out don't count on it.
   - **Fixed here:** the feed status could read a poll stamped later than "now" as proof the feed was alive.
26. **The bell show** (done).
   - **The Tower step:** a new `runBell` step, run once per bell after the gm chains, scores anything outstanding and reveals every market that settled that day (one settling just after the bell waits for the next). Receipts become moods: CALLED IT, and REKT with its free Copium. 4+ REKT squares on the day's grid tilt you; small misses (white squares) and calls from before the Slate went out don't count.
   - **Crew Bell Scores:** one chip per CALLED IT by a current crewmate, ×1.5 per Long Shot caught (a YES that hit), ×1.25 for a 7+ day gm chain. The ×2 for a won Snap waits for Fade Duels.
   - **Bell Stamps:** one tap after a locked call that day pays 25 GM (150 on Sundays), once, even with racing taps.
   - **The reel's data:** the Slate band by band with the Long Shot last, the top 3 crews and yours, the day's burn, your grid, and tomorrow's Long Shot. Only after the bell.
   - **Waits for later bricks:** the foil stamp for being on the Roof at the bell, the losing Bunkers' lights, the Ratio of the Day and Tower Lights.
27. **The cast** (done).
   - **Accounts:** NOTHING-9000, the Contrarian, Faucet Fren and Hash Brown are seeded by migration with fixed ids and a "NPC · AI" label on every call, receipt, Right List row and Board line. They have no resident and can never hold a GM account (a database trigger refuses one).
   - **Their calls:** NOTHING-9000 says NO to every Slate card as soon as it's out; the Contrarian calls at 12:00 UTC against where the city leans (or against the world's favourite when nobody has called). They lock like anyone's but never record a streak, never move the City or Crew bars, never get moods, tilt or Bell Score chips, never count in a grid percentile, and fading one pings nobody. They never call a frozen card.
   - **Board lines:** after the bell each posts one templated line on its home Board, at most 3 NPC lines a Board a day ("Nothing happened. Again."). Hash Brown's alpha is always fiction, and every line passes the NPC rules.
28. **The Daily Close** (done).
   - **Printing:** the city paper is printed once per bell (the last `runBell` step) from game data, as template keys and values ready for translation.
   - **Pages:** a front page (the Long Shot, hit or missed, and the biggest Vibe Gap among cards with 10+ callers and how it settled), City Hall (the Law of the Week, naming the Mayor), Crews (top 3 Bell Scores), the cast (each NPC's line plus the feud of the day) and the weather (green candles, Copium, REKT).
   - **Your page:** a headline from your own real Slate day ("Local Roof Rat Fades City, Lives." only when going against the city actually won).
   - **Who's named:** only the Mayor and crews, never a resident's id or handle. The cast's calls never move the city's numbers.
29. **Fade Duels** (done; creating one is behind `FEATURE_DUELS`, off until the alt checks are live, as the plan says).
   - **Stakes:** a GM side pot between two residents on opposite sides of a market that settles by the next bell. 10 GM each, a Snap each doubles it, and Last Call at 23:00 doubles it once more (80 each at most). Each duel has its own escrow, and every move goes through the ledger under a lock on the duel row.
   - **Outcomes:** the winner takes the pot (no GM created); Retreat before the lock gives the opponent your ante and burns the rest; a voided market or an unanswered offer gives the stakes back. A lost Snap tilts you.
   - **Guards:** opt-in both ways, Proof of Neighbour, 300 GM, 3 open at a time (checked under locks), one per pair a week (declines count), never the cast, never across a block, no Snap while tilted, and a Snap the other side can't cover is refused with nothing moved.
   - **At the bell:** duels settle at the bell; a won Snap doubles the crew's Bell Score, and the reel and the Daily Close print "@a ate @b's Snap", naming only residents who allow it.
   - **Tests:** every scenario checks that no GM is created or lost.
30. **The Grudge Match** (done). The one duel a newcomer can play from minute one: against the friend whose crew invite they redeemed (now recorded, first inviter wins), head to head on tomorrow's Slate. 10 GM each in its own escrow, no Snap, once per pair ever in either direction (a unique index, so racing offers make one match), never across a block. Settled at that day's bell only: higher Slate Aura takes the 20 GM pot, a tie gives both back, an unanswered offer goes home.
   - Also fixed: a brick 23 race test whose barrier locked a row that didn't exist yet (flaky), and a test that assumed which Coin Flip sits in which Slate slot.
31. **Only neighbours move the city's numbers** (done). Per the plan, calls from accounts without Proof of Neighbour never move the City bar or the Aura boards, so one viral link can't brigade a market. One helper (`cityCalls`) now feeds the City bar, receipts, the reel and the paper's Vibe Gap with Proof of Neighbour calls only (never the cast), and the Right List ranks only Proof of Neighbour residents (the cast keeps its labelled rows). The crew bar is unchanged.
32. **City markets and the Season Stack** (done).
   - **The markets:** staff write them (a headcount like "20+ on the Roof at the bell?", or a manual one staff resolve), lock 2 hours before the bell, settle at the bell. Residents stake GM once per market with a ticket, inside a cap of 25% of their GM, 100 + 10 a day as a resident (800 at most), 200 before Proof of Neighbour, and 10 while tilted. The cap is checked under a per-resident lock, so two stakes at once can't share a balance.
   - **Payouts:** winners split the losers' stakes by stake, minus the 2% bell burn on wins over 50 (capped at 500), and rounding dust burns. Paid + burned = staked (property-tested), and a winner never loses GM. One-sided or voided markets refund everyone; an unresolved manual market waits for a later bell.
   - **Headcounts:** count Proof of Neighbour residents who made a call that day and were in the room at the moment, never away residents or the cast.
   - **Season Stack:** GM profit on City markets and Fade Duels this season, counted by the market's bell day. City results open the reel.

## Deploying

1. Postgres (Neon, Supabase or RDS), then `DATABASE_URL=... pnpm db:migrate`.
2. `wrangler hyperdrive create` against it; put the id in `apps/api/wrangler.jsonc`.
3. Secrets on both Workers (`wrangler secret put`): `INTERNAL_SECRET` and `TICKET_SECRET` (the same on both), plus the API's `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`, and when ready `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `PRIVY_APP_ID`, `PRIVY_VERIFICATION_KEY`.
4. `wrangler deploy` in `apps/rooms`, then in `apps/api` (each runs its own build). Check `/health` says the schema is up to date.

## Notes for later bricks

- **Rent vs open stakes.** Rent's "can't pay" check reads the wallet balance. When duels and City/Crew markets add escrowed stakes, GM parked in a stake over a Saturday could dodge rent. Count open stakes there the same way `gmNetWorth` will.
- **The bell job** must run `collectAllRent` at the Saturday bell before players can act, and `settleCrewChain` for every crew each night.
- **Hot system rows (scale).** Mints and burns are sharded (brick 20). Still single rows: `sys:city_purse` (rent's 10%, Saturdays only) and each crew pot. Load-test the whole API with bots before the open launch.
- **Sharding and old idempotency keys.** A posting's idempotency hash includes its account ids, so retrying a mint or burn that was first posted before sharding (to a resident now on shard 1 to 15) is refused as a conflict. Harmless before launch; never re-shard a live database without a plan for in-flight retries.
- **Mood hooks.** The bell grants Called It and REKT (`grantMood`); grids, Snaps and daily losses call `maybeTilt`; rooms call `grantMood(cozy)` and `endCozy`; a nap in your own bed grants Rested. Stakes and real trades must read `moodState(...).tilt` before accepting.
- **Voting in person.** Now live: the API reads City Hall presence from the rooms Worker. Ballot secrecy holds against players and staff tools; a database superuser reading Postgres internals could still infer the most recent vote.
- **Moderation wiring.** Chat must run `checkMessage` before sending, `enqueueModeration` after, attach evidence from stored messages when filing reports, and check `isRestricted(chat_mute)`, `isBlockedEitherWay`, `isMuted` and `allowNewDmThread`. The profanity list is empty by default: choose a maintained list and pass it as `blockedWords`. Set `OPENAI_API_KEY` and run `/internal/moderation/run` on a cron.
- **Away list at scale.** `awayAt` scans residents seen in the last 7 days on each call (rooms cache it 15 minutes). Fine for the beta; before launch, precompute per place per slot.
- **Notification hooks.** Later bricks call `notify(...)` for their events (faded, Snap, syndicate shake, warm-up settled, shift done). The daily scheduler (brick 22) pings for live streaks; once calls exist, add "something of theirs is riding" as a reason, and note streaks only advance when the calls brick calls `recordStreakDay`. The crew bot calls `crewBotPostAllowed` before posting. Telegram only delivers to residents who have started the bot; others come back as `no_channel`.
- **Proof of Neighbour providers.** Plug in a phone verifier (Twilio Verify or similar), crypto-proof verifiers (Alchemy for wallet age, EAS on Base for Coinbase Verification, World ID) and a `PON_PEPPER`. Until then the routes answer 503.

## Testing rules

- Real Postgres, never mocks.
- Race tests use a lock barrier (`race()` in `accounts.test.ts`) that waits until every racer is provably blocked in Postgres. Plain `Promise.all` races ran one after another on a warm pool and hid bugs.
- Each guard gets a mutation check: break it on purpose and confirm a test goes red.
- `api-contract.test.ts` calls every exported service with garbage and fails on any synchronous throw (this bug shipped twice).
- Never let randomness make a test pass by luck: seed it or pin it.
- Check database errors by SQLSTATE on `error.cause` (Drizzle wraps them), and never turn an unknown error into a friendly one.
- Every open-ended loop carries a `loop-bound:` comment saying what limits it; `loops.test.ts` fails the build otherwise.
- Exploit loops get property tests: random sequences of real actions must never beat a cap.
- Row locks are `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, never plain `FOR UPDATE` (it blocks foreign-key checks and deadlocked twice); `locks.test.ts` enforces it. Read a balance you are about to post against through `lockAccounts`.
- A fuzzer must prove it reaches the handlers: ours once logged itself out and tested nothing but 401s.
- Lock order everywhere: resident, then request/invite, then crew, then ledger accounts.
- Shared helpers live in `packages/db/test/helpers.ts`. Test files share one database, so assertions must be scoped to their own rows.
